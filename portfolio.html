Engineering & Systems

Projects where the interesting part is the engine underneath.

FEA Online — finite-element analysis in the browser 2026  Open source
Solo ♦ github.com/gitnlsn/fea-online
  • Draw a geometry, mesh it and solve on it — all client-side. The mesher and a discontinuous Galerkin solver are written in Rust and compiled to WebAssembly, running in a Web Worker.
  • Solves steady heat diffusion with per-edge boundary conditions (plotted in 2D or as a 3D surface) and transient compressible Euler — shock waves reflecting and diffracting around the drawn geometry.
  • The mesher, nlsn-delaunay-refine (constrained Delaunay triangulation and refinement), is published separately on crates.io.
Stack: Rust, WebAssembly (wasm-bindgen), TypeScript, Next.js, Web Workers, Canvas
